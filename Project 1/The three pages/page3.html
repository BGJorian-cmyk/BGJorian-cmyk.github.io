My Favorte Video Game

My favorite videogame that I like to play is "Minecraft" a sandbox game where you can build and survive. You can create tour own worlds and either set the mode to survival where you try to survive and gather materials to beat this monster named the "Ender Dragon" or go in creative and do build and fly around the world and just do your own thing without worrying about dying. I play Minecraft using my PS5

Why Minecraft?

The reasons why Minecraft is my favorite game to play is because the survival mode can be challenging and annoying, but fun especially with other players. Another reason Minecraft is my favorite game is because I mostly play in creative mode and I like to explore what Minecraft has to offer while not being able to die. When I'm in creative mode, I like to test some features, build things and explore. I also like to play Minecraft with my friends and cousin. I remember when during the pandemic, I would play Minecraft a lot, especially with my cousin.

A fun different way to play

There are special ways to play Minecraft and they are special worlds and addons in minecraft. Addons are special features you can by in the Minecraft shop with minecoins to change up the gameplay a little. However, you can only buy minecoins with real money. There isn't a way you can earn them in the game currently. For the Minecraft worlds. They are unique and have twists that make them interesting. Some of them also have their own stories. There were some to showcase some of the new updates that came out. I enjoyed those unique ways to play minecraft. One of my favorite addons is the one when there are added animations for mobs and items called "Actions & Stuff" I like how they made the mobs and players more expressive and lively in the addon. One of my favorie worlds is the 15 anniversary world. It celebrates the 15th anniversary of Minecraft and it's history throughout the years. You can also find stickers and play some mini-games for them. Some of the stickers were hard to find, but they were fun to find There were also some fun collab worlds like Pac-Man and Spongebob. Overall, despite the fact that you need to pay real money to play worlds and addons, some of them are free and the worlds and addons are fun to play on.
