Jorian's Journey in game design

My name is Jorian Houston. I am a second year game design and experential media major at the Illinois Institute Of Technology. My home page is about my experiences when working on game design like my classes, projects and other extra activities. In the future, I would like to work on any type of video game like console or mobile games.

Game design projects

The first project I want to talk about was a choose your own adventure game that I helped create for a group project on Twine called "Voices of the Zibe." It was about two main characters meeting a tribe and interacting with them and Positive interactions give "fellowship points" and negative interactions takes some away. THe amount you have determines if you get the good, neutral, or bad ending. I was responsible for typing up the codes for the different scenereos if the main characters wanted to do something. This was my first experience with Twine. We presented our game near the end of the semester

The second project I wanted to mention was another Twine game I made for a game jam with three other people. This time, the game was called "Greif on a Leaf." It was about a girl who's little sister passed away in a car crash and turned into a pile of leaves and years later, the girl is at a halloween party and the friends split off and she soon goes with each of them and how she treats them decides their fate. The amount of friends alive determined on a good, neutral and bad ending. I was responsible for writing a portion if the game this time. We submitted it for the game jam and it was one of the winners for first place.
